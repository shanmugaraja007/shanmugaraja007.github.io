Research

My work sits at the intersection of perception, geometry and simulation. I care about minimum sensor results: how much of a scene you can rebuild from the cheapest instrument on the vehicle, and how much of the remaining error is calibration and timing rather than the model.

Monocular Bird's-Eye-View Reconstruction of Off-Road Field Scenarios for Re-Simulation

Shanmugaraja Pandiyan

MSc dissertation, BSBI Berlin · conducted at IAV GmbH, 2026

Can one uncalibrated forward looking camera on a working agricultural machine give you enough to rebuild the field it drove through, well enough to replay in a simulator? Semantic segmentation into terrain, grass and trees, vertical class collapse so tall structure stops smearing across the ground plane, inverse perspective projection into a metric BEV at 0.10 m per pixel, monocular visual odometry with keyframe selection, confidence weighted multi view accumulation, and rectangle decomposition into simulator ready terrain patches.

Along the way: a systematic 32 percent scale error traced back to a camera height recorded from the axle rather than the ground, a 6.7 second GPS time offset that inflated apparent trajectory error by an order of magnitude, and a 32 bit latitude field in a J1939 message the documentation described as 31. Most of the accuracy came from calibration and timing discipline, not from the model.

Monocular BEV Reconstruction Pipeline

Autonomous driving re-simulation team

IAV GmbH, 2026

The research pipeline as production code: swappable segmentation backends, depth weighted voting into a global accumulator, motion based keyframe selection, and automatic chaining into terrain patch creation. Runs a full sequence in about 30 minutes on a 4 GB NVIDIA A2000, with FP16 and batching projected to bring that to 5 to 8 minutes.

Trajectory Evaluation Toolkit

Benchmarking against public off road data

IAV GmbH, 2026

Benchmarking with no ROS install anywhere in the loop. Reads bag files directly, decodes NMEA GPS, projects to UTM zone 32N with 0.03 m round trip error, and aligns estimated against ground truth trajectories with Umeyama to report ATE, RPE and scale.
